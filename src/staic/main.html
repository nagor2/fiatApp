What is DotFlat?

DotFlat is a fully collateralized stablecoin with permanent purchasing power, running on Ethereum mainnet.

How can I use it?

You can use DotFlat to transfer value on the Blockchain, protect your savings from inflation, and earn interest.

Introduction

Originally, Blockchain was a way to avoid control over value transfers and possession, but native crypto like Bitcoin and Ethereum is volatile. Today’s stablecoins have several severe weak points: non-transparent collateral if any, ability to block your assets, inflation of the indicative currency, and tight interconnection with the traditional financial system and banks. Sounds inconvenient, doesn’t it?

How DFC works?

DotFlat is a fully collateralized and fully decentralized coin, pegged to a commodities index using cross-rates, so its value stays stable relative to average commodity prices — silver, gold, copper, orange juice, coffee, corn, wheat, cotton, rice, lumber, oil, gas, cattle, and so on. Think of it as a weighted basket of vital goods.

DotFlat (DFC) uses credit emission: each coin is minted through a smart contract (Collateral Debt Position, or CDP) to a user who has provided sufficient collateral in ETH, according to the current commodities basket price and ETH/USD quotes. If the price of ETH rises against commodities, each coin stays fully collateralized. If not, the system requires the user to increase their collateral — and if that doesn’t happen, sells the collateral through an auction to cover the emission made by that user.

At launch, the price of each DFC was set equal to one US dollar. But as USD suffers inflation — which drives up the price of commodities — DFC preserves purchasing power and appreciates against USD alongside an average commodity. From time to time DFC may even be cheaper than a US dollar, when commodity prices fall. You can always check the indicative price on this page.

Does it matter if DFC falls in dollar terms?

No — and that is the point. If DFC drops against the dollar, it simply means commodities got cheaper. You can still buy the same basket of goods with your DFC as you could before. The absolute dollar price is irrelevant; what matters is what you can actually purchase. A dollar that silently loses 4% a year looks stable on the screen while your savings quietly erode. DFC doesn’t pretend — it tracks reality.

Advantages

  • Fully collateralized – each coin minted is backed by ETH placed on the smart contract’s balance
  • Fully decentralized – the system is controlled by smart contracts with open, verified code
  • Totally transparent – all balances, terms, quotes, transactions and collaterals can be viewed on the Blockchain
  • Inflation-proof – you can buy the same amount of goods in fifty years if you keep your value in DFC
  • Uncensorable – no organization or group of people can freeze or affect your balance

Tokenomics

All profits from the pre-seed and pre-sales rounds go to development and marketing.

Meaning of RLE

Rule is the governance and voting token of the DFC ecosystem. All crucial parameters of the system may be changed after launch through a voting process. Each RLE counts as one vote.

Burning

The DotFlat ecosystem maintains a stability fund held at the CDP contract address — you can always verify its balance on the Blockchain. The fund must equal 5% of total DFC emission. As interest on credits or margin-call fees are paid, they go directly into the stability fund. When the fund overflows, any user can trigger an automatic auction to buy back RLE. All RLE obtained through buy-back auctions is burned, exerting upward pressure on the RLE price.

Additional RLE emission

If the stability fund falls below 5% of total supply, anyone can initiate an auction to source DFC from the market. The auction winner is rewarded with newly issued RLE. This means RLE holders are incentivized to supply DFC themselves rather than allow additional emission.

Conclusion

DotFlat is an attempt to build money that works the way money should: stable in terms of what it can buy, not in terms of a government-issued unit that inflates away. All smart contract code is verified — you can read every line of logic and confirm there is no trick.

The only current weak point is centralized commodity quotes delivery to the Blockchain. All quotes are sourced from investing.com. Decentralization of the oracle is on the roadmap.

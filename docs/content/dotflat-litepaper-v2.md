# DotFlat Light Paper v2

## What is DotFlat?

DotFlat is a collateralized stablecoin protocol on Ethereum mainnet. Its native token, DFC (DotFlat Coin), is pegged to a basket of 34 real commodity prices — gold, crude oil, natural gas, wheat, copper, and others — rather than the US dollar.

The purpose of DFC is to maintain stable purchasing power. While dollar-pegged stablecoins lose value as inflation erodes the dollar, DFC is anchored to the things the dollar buys. When commodity prices rise, DFC rises with them.

---

## The Problem

Every major stablecoin today is pegged to the US dollar: USDC, DAI, LUSD. This solves short-term volatility but creates a different problem — long-term purchasing power erosion. The dollar has lost approximately 20% of its purchasing power since 2020.

A stablecoin denominated in dollars is not stable in real terms. It is stable in nominal terms only.

DotFlat addresses this by replacing the dollar peg with a commodity basket peg.

---

## How It Works

### Oracle

DotFlat operates an on-chain oracle that tracks 34 commodity price feeds and pushes them to Ethereum mainnet continuously. Every price update is a verifiable transaction on Ethereum.

Current feeds include:
- **Precious metals:** Gold (XAU), Silver (XAG), Platinum, Palladium
- **Energy:** Crude Oil WTI, Brent Crude, Natural Gas, Heating Oil, Gasoline RBOB, London Gas Oil
- **Base metals:** Copper (COMEX), Copper (London), Aluminum, Zinc, Nickel
- **Grains:** Wheat, Corn, Soybeans, Soybean Oil, Soybean Meal, Oats, Rough Rice
- **Softs:** Coffee, Sugar, Cocoa, Cotton, Orange Juice, Lumber
- **Livestock:** Live Cattle, Lean Hogs, Feeder Cattle

The oracle node is open-source. Decentralized multi-node operation with permissionless participation is on the roadmap.

### CDP — Collateralized Debt Positions

To mint DFC, a user deposits ETH as collateral into a smart contract (CDP). The system is overcollateralized: the value of ETH collateral must exceed the value of DFC minted by a required ratio. This mechanism is identical to MakerDAO's model, with DFC tracking commodity purchasing power instead of USD.

If the collateral ratio drops below the required threshold, the position is eligible for liquidation. Liquidations are executed on-chain via an auction mechanism.

### Yield Deposits

DFC holders can lock their tokens in the Deposit contract to earn interest. Interest is paid from protocol fees generated by CDP borrowers. Deposits are tracked on-chain with no custody risk.

### Uniswap V4 Pool

DFC is tradeable on Uniswap V4 (Ethereum mainnet) in the DFC/ETH pool. Users can buy or sell DFC directly without opening a CDP position.

### Governance

RLE is a governance and voting token. All system parameters after initial deployment are changed by on-chain voting. Each RLE token equals one vote.

### Stabilization Fund

The protocol maintains a stabilization fund equal to 5% of outstanding DFC issuance, replenished by:
- Interest on CDP positions (9% per annum)
- Liquidation penalties (13%)

When the stabilization fund exceeds its target, the surplus is used to buy back RLE tokens through an auction. All RLE received by the protocol via auction is burned according to the smart contract.

### Additional Emission

If the stabilization fund is insufficient to pay deposit interest, the protocol can initiate an auction for additional RLE emission to replenish the fund. The maximum emission per auction is 1% of total outstanding supply.
